About XVOX

XVOX is an offline music player designed around immersive artwork, smooth playback, and a refined interface. Enjoy your personal music library with thoughtful controls and extensive customization, without depending on music streaming services.

Features

- Music Library — Browse, search, and organize your local music.
- Now Playing — Immersive artwork, playback controls, queue management, and lyrics.
- Mini Player — Keep your music within reach while navigating the app.
- Playlists & Favorites — Create playlists, like tracks, and revisit recently played music.
- Personalization — Customize themes, colors, typography, layouts, and navigation.
- Audio Controls — Equalizer, crossfade, and supported sound enhancements.
- Home Screen Widget — Control playback directly from your home screen.
- Background Playback — Keep listening while using other apps.
- Artwork Experience — Dynamic colors and track-specific cover art.
